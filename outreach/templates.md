# Founder Outreach Messages

These drafts are for one-to-one personalization. They are not approved for sending until the campaign owner explicitly confirms the prospect batch and copy.

## Initial message

Hi {{first_name}} — I saw that you built {{product}} with {{tool}}. {{specific_observation}}

I run a focused 24-hour launch QA audit for AI-built apps: critical flows, mobile/cross-browser checks, UX/error handling, and an evidence-backed launch recommendation.

I’m opening three founding-pilot slots at $199. If {{product}} has a current build you want independently checked, I can first confirm the scope asynchronously—no sales call required. Would that be useful?

## Short public reply

Congrats on shipping {{product}}. I noticed {{specific_observation}} and would be happy to independently check the launch-critical flows before your next push. I’m testing a fixed-scope 24-hour QA audit for AI-built apps; if useful, I can send the exact coverage and a verified sample report.

## Follow-up after three to four days

Quick follow-up, {{first_name}} — I know launch weeks are noisy. If QA is not a priority now, no problem. If it is, send the public/staging URL and the one flow you most worry about; I’ll reply with a yes/no scope decision and the exact $199 pilot coverage.

## When a founder asks what they receive

You receive a prioritized bug list with reproduction steps and screenshots/traces, browser/device coverage, a 0–100 launch-readiness score, and a clear READY / LAUNCH WITH CONDITIONS / DO NOT LAUNCH recommendation. The pilot covers up to five critical flows and includes a short walkthrough plus one verification pass. Here is the verified sample: {{sample_report_url}}.

## When a founder asks why it is paid

The $199 founding price is deliberately below the planned standard price, but it is paid because the experiment is testing real demand—not interest in free feedback. Scope is confirmed before payment, and if I cannot perform the agreed audit I refund it rather than publish an incomplete result.

## Post-pilot feedback request

Thanks again for trusting me with {{product}}. I’m improving this around outcomes, not report volume. Which finding changed your launch plan, what was hardest to act on, and would you pay for this again before another release? If the audit was useful, may I quote your answer with your name and product?

## Personalization checklist

Before sending any message:

- verify the product is still reachable;
- reference one real product or launch detail;
- state the AI-building tool only when the public source supports it;
- avoid claiming a defect before testing;
- do not imply endorsement by Product Hunt or the AI-building platform;
- use the founder’s public contact channel and respect its rules;
- record the message and date in the tracker.
